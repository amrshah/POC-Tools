Management conclusion

Price the implementation and the five-year service obligation separately.

The project needs a temporary multidisciplinary delivery team for discovery, design, build, migration, accessibility, testing and launch. After go-live, that team should reduce to a small operational function for application support, monitoring, security maintenance, incident response and disaster-recovery readiness. The model below keeps these staffing pools separate so the five-year price is not inflated by carrying the full implementation team into operations—or understated by ignoring ongoing obligations.

Project execution roadmap

A six-month implementation plan with overlapping workstreams, followed by 60 months of managed operations (months 7–66). Phase durations are planning windows, not additive effort estimates.

PHASE 01 · WEEKS 1–3

Mobilization & discovery

Kick-off, stakeholder interviews, current-site audit, requirements, analytics, integrations register, risks and acceptance criteria.

3 weeks
PHASE 02 · WEEKS 2–6

Content inventory & information architecture

Count pages, PDFs and page counts, Office files, forms and media; define taxonomy, navigation, content types, migration rules and archive priorities.

5 weeks · overlaps discovery
PHASE 03 · WEEKS 4–9

UX, design & accessibility planning

Resident/staff journeys, responsive design system, page templates, WCAG 2.1 AA criteria and accessible-document approach.

6 weeks
PHASE 04 · WEEKS 7–17

CMS platform build & integrations

CMS setup, templates, permissions, search, forms, integrations, hosting, CI/CD, security controls, backups and monitoring.

11 weeks
PHASE 05 · WEEKS 10–21

Migration & remediation

Content mapping, data entry/import, PDF and document triage, accessibility remediation, link checks, deduplication and sample-based reconciliation.

12 weeks · archive-dependent
PHASE 06 · WEEKS 18–24

QA, UAT, training & launch

Functional, browser, performance, security and accessibility testing; acceptance, editor training, launch rehearsal, go-live and hypercare.

7 weeks
PHASE 07 · MONTHS 7–66

Managed operations

Monitoring, patching, backup verification, recovery testing, incident response, CMS support, security maintenance, reporting and controlled change requests.

60 months after implementation
Critical migration dependency: The District will not publish a fixed inventory. Q79 includes a bidder estimate of approximately 425 webpages and 127 linked PDFs; it is not a District-certified count. PDF page count, scan quality, tables, forms, accessibility defects, duplicates, broken links and approvals determine effort. Board records remain on CivicWeb. Obtain a verified crawl and representative document sample before treating the migration allowance as fixed.
